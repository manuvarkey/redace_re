## Smoother busy missions on phones

Missions with many aircraft and anti-aircraft guns, such as the demo mission, could drop frames on Android
while Training 1 ran smoothly. Graphics settings made no difference because the time went into the game's own
checks: every plane tested every building and tree on the map, every physics step, to see whether it had
flown into one, and every anti-aircraft gun searched the whole map for a target.

- **Collision checks** now look only at the part of the map each plane is passing over, as the original game
  did with RenderWare's world sectors.
- **Target searches** by anti-aircraft guns, the AI and the minimap use per-group lists, as the original game did.

The game's CPU time per physics step falls about sixfold in the demo mission (6.6 ms to 1.1 ms on a desktop
CPU) and in Mission 1 (4.8 ms to 0.7 ms). Collisions, targeting and AI give the same results as before:
a new test compares the new lookups with the old full scans over every step of seven missions.

The original 28 missions, the Red Baron campaign and their rules are unchanged.

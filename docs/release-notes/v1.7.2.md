## Game controllers on phones, and a working load bar

- **Controllers on Android and phone browsers:** with a game controller connected and the Joystick layout chosen
  (Options > Controls, "Set to default for: Joystick"), the controller's sticks and throttle now fly the plane.
  Before, the touch controls took every axis and only the controller's buttons worked. A finger on the on-screen
  stick or the THR slider still takes over while held, and the touch buttons keep working. Without a controller,
  touch and tilt steering work as before.
- **Gyroscope option on phones:** the Controls page's "Gyroscope?" box now works on phones, steering by the phone's
  own tilt while the stick is at rest. This suits a phone clipped to its controller. CAL recentres it. On desktop
  it still uses the controller's motion sensors.
- **Loading bar:** the loading page's bar now fills as the mission loads, one segment per 5 %, as the original
  draws it.

The original 28 missions, the Red Baron campaign and their rules are unchanged.

## Der Rote Kampfflieger: a new Red Baron campaign

A twelve-mission story campaign flown as Manfred von Richthofen, from his duel with Major Lanoe Hawker in
November 1916 to April 1918. Find it under **Goodies → Bonus Missions → Der Rote Kampfflieger**.

- **Twelve missions** on the country and trenches maps: Hawker, First Sortie, Bloody April, Scramble, The Plates,
  Balloon Busting, The Other Side, Wounded, Dusk Raiders, Cambrai, Formation and The Last Patrol.
- **A story told in the cockpit.** You teach Leutnant Brenner to fly as part of a squadron, and later Brenner's
  young second, Hartwig, and the new pilots of 1918. Each mission has a period briefing, a debriefing and its own
  failure page. Brenner's fate in Mission 11 is shown in a short camera cut-in. The campaign ends in a scripted
  closing sequence and an epilogue.
- **New aircraft and objects:** Albatros D.II/D.III/D.V and Fokker Dr.I in squadron colours, the R.E.8, F.E.2d,
  Sopwith Camel, S.E.5a, Halberstadt, Handley Page O/100 (with nose, dorsal and ventral gunners), Caquot
  observation balloons, British tanks and a working rail yard. Dawn and dusk skies.
- **Clear objectives.** HUD lines show the current objective and progress. The minimap marks targets in red,
  what you must protect in green and the aerodrome. Warnings and objective changes have their own sounds.
- **Campaign progress per pilot,** with promotions from Leutnant to Rittmeister. A **Replay Missions** list opens
  as you go, and all twelve missions are listed once the campaign is complete.
- **Painted campaign artwork** for the briefings, the campaign pages, the letter in Mission 11 and the epilogue.

## Also new

- **Graphics options:** an independent render resolution, MSAA antialiasing, anisotropic filtering, and
  water, cloud and scenery detail settings.
- **Faster bullets:** less collision and tracer overhead in busy fights.
- **Controls:** joystick pitch is always direct (Quick and Gentle now shape roll only); controller tilt keeps
  steering after the stick has been moved.
- **Mods:** mod campaigns appear in the menus with their own pages, progress and replay list. Missions can have
  their own debriefing and failure pages, minimap markers and scripted events.
- **Battle of Britain bonus mission:** a stall ceiling at altitude, so the Spitfire can no longer climb without limit.
- **Fixes:** the trenches minimap is drawn from the original map image. Settings tests no longer touch your saved
  pilots.

The original 28 missions and their rules are unchanged.

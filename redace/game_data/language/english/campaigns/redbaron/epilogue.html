The four pilots returned to the aerodrome. Brenner's second waited beside his aircraft until the others had landed.
 
Their commander did not return.
 
 
Historical note
 
Rittmeister Manfred Freiherr von Richthofen fell on 21 April 1918 near Vaux-sur-Somme. He was buried the next day at Bertangles by his enemies, No. 3 Squadron of the Australian Flying Corps, with full military honours. A wreath from the Allied airmen read: "To our gallant and worthy foe."

Der Rote Kampfflieger

November 1916. The war in the air is two years old, and the German Air Service is learning how to fight it. You fly as Manfred von Richthofen, a young Leutnant of Jasta 2, and later as the commander of Jasta 11 and Jagdgeschwader 1.
Your rule is simple: never chase alone, never fight low over the enemy's lines. Teach it to the young pilots who fly with you. They will need it.

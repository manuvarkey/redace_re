Hints and Tips for Master of the Skies: The Red Ace

If you're under attack from an AA gun, or from enemy aircraft, take evasive action by banking left or right - if you keep going in a straight line you'll make yourself an easy target.

When bombing, drop your payload before you reach the target, as inertia will affect the way the bombs fall.

Below is information on specific levels which we are frequently asked about.

SPOILERS! - Don't look if you want to work them out for yourselves!

Mission 7
Avoid AA gunfire either by flying very high or flying very low. Some guns can only fire through an 180 degree radius, so in theory it's possible to sneak up behind them. Learn to bomb accurately. The general rule is - the higher you are, the earlier you have to release the bomb. Use the UP arrow key to view your plane from above and see how your bombs are falling.

Mission 15
A difficult one, this! A tip - gun turrets can't shoot you if you stay below their angle of elevation (ie. about 10 feet above the sea!). Also, you don't actually have to knock out all the guns to save your comrades, only the ones closest to them (though you'll have to come back for the others to complete the mission). Once you've destroyed the guns, you'll still have to escort the friendly planes off the map - there's usually at least one enemy waiting for them before they escape. Phew!

Mission 19
You can't actually kill the Baron on this level - he's just there to taunt you. You may need to hit the oil wells several times before they register as 'destroyed' and you can complete the level.

Mission 24
This is basically a test of all the dogfighting skills you've learnt in the course of the game, so there's no one strategy to adopt - just shoot them all down before they get you. Don't worry about losing your wingman - unfortunately there's nothing you can do to save him.

Mission 25
You're on your own with this one!

The First World War was the first war which featured airborne combat, shortly after the birth of flight itself.
Planes initially were sent on scouting missions, but once scouting aircraft from opposing sides met each other in the skies, the navigators started taking potshots at each other by throwing bricks and firing pistols.
Shortly afterwards guns became mounted on planes, and the first true fighter planes were born.
You are a fighter pilot flying these early planes for the allied forces against the enemy side. We are searching for the most deadly of enemy pilots, the Red Baron.

THIS IS A TRAINING MISSION ONLY
You start in the air - use ^controlthrottleup^ and ^controlthrottledown^ to adjust your throttle. Beware lowering your speed too much as you may stall your plane.
Use ^controlleft^ and ^controlright^ to roll your plane, and ^controldown^ and ^controlup^ to change its pitch.
Shoot the Enemy Fighters.
^controlfire1^ fires machine guns. Enemy fighters are marked on the map (top right corner of screen) as black and white crosses. Your position on the map is shown as a green dot.
Use the camera controls to change your viewpoint.
Good luck pilot!

THIS IS A TRAINING MISSION ONLY
You begin on an airstrip. Use ^controlthrottleup^ to increase your throttle (top left corner of screen) to maximum. When you have gathered enough speed pull back on the mouse to become airborne.
Take-off from the runway and Bomb the Ground Targets.
Use ^controlfire2^ releases bombs - you have 35 on board. Release bombs slightly before you reach your target as they have inertia. Do not fly too low while bombing - you may get caught in your own bomb blast. Each target will require more than one bomb drop - you may want to weaken the targets with your machine guns, using ^controlfire1^.
^controlcamdown^ provides the most useful camera angle for bombing.
Good luck pilot!

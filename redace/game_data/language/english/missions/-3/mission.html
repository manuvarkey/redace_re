Welcome pilot,
You are not alone - allied fighters are shown on the map as blue circles, enemy fighters are shown as black and white crosses, and ground targets as red dots. You must destroy the ground targets. Don't worry about anti-aircraft guns on the ground - try to avoid their fire, be patient, and your allies will take them out.
Objective : Destroy the factories and enemy fighters.
Controls:
Roll: ^controlleft^ and ^controlright^
Pitch: ^controlup^ and ^controldown^
Throttle: ^controlthrottleup^ and ^controlthrottledown^
Fire: ^controlfire1^ Bomb: ^controlfire2^
If you can save your buddy, being chased by an enemy to your right as you begin, he will reward you by helping with your bombing campaign!

Hawker
Jasta 2, 23 November 1916.

23 November 1916. A lone British DH.2 has crossed the lines. Its pilot is Major Lanoe Hawker VC, one of the bravest men the British have. Meet him with respect, and with patience.
You fly an Albatros D.II.
Objective : Shoot down Major Hawker's DH.2.

He turns tighter than you, but your Albatros is faster. Keep your height, wait for a clean shot, and cut him off if he runs for his lines.

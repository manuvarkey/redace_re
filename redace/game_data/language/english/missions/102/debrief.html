January 1917. You brought Brenner home. He sulked through dinner, then asked you to show him the turn you used on the Scout. The courage was always there; now he is learning discipline. That is how a squadron begins.

The machines are being painted red, all of them. Let the British know who they are fighting. Spring is coming, and with it their offensive at Arras.^promotionnote^

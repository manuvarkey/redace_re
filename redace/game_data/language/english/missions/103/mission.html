Bloody April
Jasta 11, April 1917.

April 1917. Three British spotters are directing artillery onto our batteries. Every minute they stay up, gunners below are dying. The spotter crews are brave men doing their duty; so are ours. Bring them down before the next barrage.
Objective : Shoot down all three spotters within four minutes.

The spotters are marked red. Two Nieuports cover them, and the observers fire back: never sit straight behind a two-seater.

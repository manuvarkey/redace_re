June 1917. Three balloons burned before the sun was fully up, and the British guns are blind for a day, perhaps two. It is grim work, and you said so. But every hour they cannot see is an hour our infantry are not shelled, and the Halberstadt crews flew into those guns to give you your chance.

Tomorrow the squadron flies the other side of the bargain: escort for one of our own reconnaissance machines.^promotionnote^

Balloon Busting
Jasta 11, June 1917, dawn.

June 1917, dawn. Before their offensive the British must be blinded. Three observation balloons hang over their lines, ringed by guns. Our Halberstadts will bomb the guns on the near side of each balloon, then turn for home.
Objective : Burn all three balloons.

Go in with the bombing or soon after: the longer you wait, the more British scouts come up, and the far guns keep firing. Dive in, burn, get out.

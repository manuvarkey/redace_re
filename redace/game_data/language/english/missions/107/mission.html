The Other Side
Jasta 11, June 1917.

A Halberstadt crew will photograph the British lines today, and you are their escort. They cannot turn to fight: their lives are in your hands. The pictures are worthless if the crew does not come home.
Objective : Bring the photo plane over the lines and home again.

The Halberstadt is marked green. Camels will come at it in pairs: on the way out, over the photo run and on the way home.

Dusk Raiders
Jasta 11, September 1917, dusk.

Handley Page bombers are coming for the rail yard that feeds the front. Every train it sends carries food, shells and men for our soldiers in the trenches. Do not let their size fool you about their guns.
Objective : Shoot down all three bombers before they wreck half the rail yard (three of its six stations, sheds and wagons).

The bombers are marked red, the yard green. Five Camels escort them: one with each bomber, two ahead to meet us. Brenner and one more of ours fly with you.

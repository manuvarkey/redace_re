Cambrai
Jasta 11, 20 November 1917.

British tanks have broken through at Cambrai and our guns cannot see them through the smoke. Our infantry are holding the village with rifles alone. Take the new Fokker triplane down there and stop the tanks.
Objective : Destroy six of the eight tanks within five minutes.

Your Dr.I carries 30 rockets. Brenner and one of the new pilots will keep the Camels off you. The tanks fire back: keep moving.

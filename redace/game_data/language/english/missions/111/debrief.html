March 1918. Leutnant Brenner did not come home. His second is safe, and the two-seater crew with him.

Madam, it is my duty to tell you that your son fell today. He died saving my life. He was the finest pilot I have trained, and I will not forget him.
Rittmeister Manfred Freiherr von Richthofen^promotionnote^

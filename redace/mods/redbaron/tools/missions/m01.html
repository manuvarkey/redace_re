Hawker
Jasta 2, 23 November 1916.

23 November 1916. A British DH.2 has crossed the lines, flown by a pilot who will not run until he must. Engage him. Patience wins this fight, not speed.
You fly an Albatros D.II.
Objective : Shoot down the DH.2.

His machine turns tighter than yours: stay above him and wait. If he gets back over his lines, he is gone.

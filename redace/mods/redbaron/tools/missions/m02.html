First Sortie
Jasta 11, January 1917.

You take command of Jasta 11. Leutnant Brenner flies on your wing today: brave, eager, and too quick to chase. Teach him the first rule of this squadron: we never fight alone, and we bring each other home.
Objective : Catch Brenner and lead him home.

If he breaks off, fly close to him to call him back. The aerodrome is marked on your map.
If Brenner is lost, the mission fails.

First Sortie
Jasta 11, January 1917.

January 1917. You take command of Jasta 11. Leutnant Brenner flies on your wing today. Teach him the first rule: we never chase alone.
Objective : Catch Brenner and lead him home.

If he breaks off, fly close to him to call him back. The aerodrome is marked on your map.
Leutnant Brenner lost: mission failed.

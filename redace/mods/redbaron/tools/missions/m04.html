Scramble
Jasta 11, May 1917.

May 1917. British bombers are coming for the aerodrome: four RE8s with bombs, three S.E.5as ahead of them. Leutnant Brenner and one of ours are already up; you are still on the field. Get airborne and protect the hangars and the men who keep us flying.
Objective : Shoot down the four bombers. Keep four of the six hangars standing.

The bombers are marked red, the hangars green. Leave the S.E.5as to Brenner if you can: the bombers are what matter.

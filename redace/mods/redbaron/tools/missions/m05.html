The Plates
Jasta 11, May 1917.

May 1917. A British reconnaissance crew has photographed our aerodrome. If those plates reach their lines, the bombers come tomorrow, and the men on the ground will pay for it. Stop the plates getting through.
Objective : Shoot down the reconnaissance two-seater.

The two-seater is marked red. Two S.E.5as escort it and its observer fires back. It is running south: if it leaves the map, the plates are through.

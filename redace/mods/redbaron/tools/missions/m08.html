Wounded
Jasta 11, 6 July 1917.

6 July 1917. A flight of F.E.2d two-seaters is over our lines. Attack. Their observers can shoot.
You fly an Albatros D.V. Leutnant Brenner flies on your wing.
Objective : Shoot down the F.E.2ds before they get home, then return to base.

The aerodrome is marked on your map.

Dusk Raiders
Jasta 11, September 1917, dusk.

September 1917, dusk. High Command has pulled the fighter squadrons back to defend the rail yards. Handley Page bombers are coming for the yard that feeds the front. They are slow and they are big. Do not let their size fool you about their guns.
Objective : Shoot down all three bombers before they wreck half the rail yard (three of its six stations, sheds and wagons).

Five Camels escort them: one sticks to each bomber, two fly out ahead to meet us. Brenner and one more of ours fly with you. The yard is marked on your map.

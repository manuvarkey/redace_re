Formation
Jasta 11, March 1918.

March 1918. A damaged reconnaissance machine is returning from the advance. Its observer is wounded, and enemy scouts are following it. Bring the two-seater home. Brenner and his young second, Leutnant Hartwig, will cover you.
Objective : Escort the damaged two-seater to the aerodrome.

The two-seater is marked green, its condition on your HUD. It flies straight for home and will not turn to fight: keep the scouts off it.

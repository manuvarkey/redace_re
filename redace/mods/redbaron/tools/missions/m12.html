The Last Patrol
Jagdgeschwader 1, 21 April 1918.

The squadron is short of experienced pilots. Today, four young men follow you over the front. Among them is Leutnant Hartwig, Brenner's surviving second. With him fly Lenz, Vogel and Albers, a week out of flying school.
 
An enemy formation is approaching your patrol. More aircraft have been sighted beyond the lines.
 
Keep the young pilots together. Bring them home.
Objective : Bring all four pilots safely across the friendly lines.
